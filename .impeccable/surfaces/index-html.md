# Surface: AmiZoo public site (index + Servicios, Planes, Nosotros, FAQ, Contacto, legales)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of companion and production animals in north Bogotá / Sabana Norte, ages 18–80. Job: understand services + prices, trust the team, tap "Agenda tu cita" (or WhatsApp). Proof: real prices, 5 authorized testimonials, UDCA team, coverage zones. Constraints: palette/fonts pinned in PRODUCT.md; 18px base; ≥48px buttons; no fabricated photos.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The clinic comes to your door. The site is graphic livery for a mobile service: wide color panels, a mango stripe, sticker badges and a route line that ties every step and zone together. It refuses the category default of stock-dog-photo hero + generic card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Turquesa panels with a thick mango stripe running edge to edge; marfil ground with faint paw-print texture; menta as soft secondary fields. Components are rounded "stickers" (24px+ radius, 2px grafito outline on badges), pill buttons (mango/grafito), price "tags" set big in Manrope 800. A dashed route line (grafito, round caps) with circular stop markers connects sequences. Illustrations: flat SVG animals in the five palette colors, grafito outlines.

STORY: Visitor sees the slogan proven by a line-up of animals of different sizes, learns AmiZoo visits the home, sees prices plainly, follows the 3-stop route of how it works, checks their barrio among the zone stops, reads neighbors' testimonials, books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width turquesa panel. Left/top: H1 "Diferentes tamaños, misma importancia." (Manrope 800, clamp 40–76px, grafito), supporting line, mango pill "Agenda tu cita" + outline "Escríbenos por WhatsApp". Bottom of panel: illustrated animal line-up standing on the mango stripe, budgie → cat → dog → sheep → cow, ascending size. Sticker badge "A domicilio · Bogotá y Sabana Norte".

FORM: Candidate 6 of 7 (mobile-service vehicle livery → "Ruta a domicilio"), seed key b1a6c600 (degraded roll, user chose it over pick "carné de vacunación"). Signature interaction: the route line draws itself as "Cómo funciona" enters view; budgie greeting flight to the bot corner on first visit.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
